Our Story

GESCOM was born from first-hand client experience

It started with a simple observation in Mauricie: many entrepreneurs and small businesses were overwhelmed by administrative tasks, keeping them from focusing on their core business.

Aurélie Genin, with years of experience in administration and commercial management, decided to put her expertise to work for the companies in Mauricie that need it most. That’s how GESCOM launched in 2020, with a clear mission: free entrepreneurs from the administrative burden.

Every client we’ve worked with in Mauricie has enriched our approach. From the first invoices to complex dashboards, from simple follow-ups to tailored support, we built our way of working around your real needs—whether you are in Trois-Rivières, Shawinigan or Saint-Élie-de-Caxton.

"Every business deserves professional administrative support, tailored to its reality and growth pace."

Aurélie Genin, Founder of GESCOM
