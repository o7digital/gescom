Administrative support in Mauricie

Emails, documents and unfinished administrative tasks take time away from running your business. I provide remote support to organize this work and handle the tasks you choose to delegate, whether you need occasional help or regular assistance.

Let’s discuss your needs

Based in Saint-Élie-de-Caxton, GESCOM provides remote support to entrepreneurs, self-employed professionals and small businesses in Mauricie, including Trois-Rivières and Shawinigan.

What I can take care of

  • Data entry and updates, document preparation and deadline tracking.
  • Professional email sorting, draft replies and follow-up on priority requests.
  • Digital filing, document scanning and organization of contracts and working documents.
  • Client and CRM updates, appointment coordination and sales follow-up.

A practical example

Customer requests are spread across several emails and it is unclear which ones still need a reply. Together, we define a filing system and tracking sheet. I bring the information together, prepare agreed replies and flag decisions for you.

Getting started

Identify the tasks taking up most of your time, the tools you use and upcoming deadlines. We then agree on the access needed, processing instructions and approvals that remain with you.

How does working together work?

  1. Together, we define the tasks you want to delegate, your priorities and deadlines.
  2. You share the necessary documents and access using the arrangements we agree on.
  3. I handle the agreed tasks and keep you informed of any points requiring your approval.

Your questions

Can I request occasional help?

Yes. Support can cover a busy period, a filing project or regular follow-up. We agree on the scope and schedule together.

Do I need to change my tools?

We start with your existing tools and procedures, then clarify the access and any adjustments needed before work begins.
